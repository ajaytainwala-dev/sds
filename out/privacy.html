Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Sridhar Driving School, Austin Town, Bengaluru

1. Overview

At Sridhar Driving School (“we”, “our”, or “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected through our website. This privacy statement explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or submit an admission enquiry.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit our online enquiry form, we may collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: Your full name.
  • Contact Information: Your mobile phone number (and email address if provided).
  • Training Preferences: Your preferred course (e.g. 4-wheeler, 2-wheeler, refresher), timing slot, and any message notes you include.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your details exclusively for legitimate business communication:

  • To contact you regarding course availability, batch timings, and current training fees.
  • To answer questions submitted through the enquiry form.
  • To provide consultation regarding Karnataka RTO licensing test requirements and schedules.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal phone numbers or names to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.

4. WhatsApp Communication

Our website provides convenience links to WhatsApp using official wa.me click-to-chat links. Please note that clicking a WhatsApp link launches the WhatsApp client on your device. Any messages sent through WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy and end-to-end encryption standards.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain admission enquiry details only as long as necessary to fulfill your enquiry and complete course enrollment. You may request the removal of your contact details from our records at any time by calling or emailing us.

6. Contact Us
