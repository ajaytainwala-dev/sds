About Sridhar Motor Driving School

Your Journey Starts Here: Safe, Calm, and Patient Driving Instruction.

Learning to drive in Bengaluru can feel intimidating with dense traffic, narrow roads, and steep flyovers. At Sridhar Motor Driving School in Austin Town, we focus on helping learners build instinctive vehicle control and road awareness in a calm, supportive environment.

Practical Fundamentals

Mastering vehicle mechanics, clutch sensitivity, and mirror habits

Austin Town Training Base: Conveniently positioned for learners across Austin Town, Neelasandra, Richmond Town, and Victoria Layout.

1-on-1 Dedicated Guidance

Every session is strictly one student per instructor. You receive focused, patient corrections without sharing behind-the-wheel time.

Dual-Control Safety Fleet

Training cars equipped with government-standard instructor dual brake and clutch controls for complete peace of mind while learning.

Bengaluru Road Mastery

Practical training covering bumper-to-bumper traffic, flyover inclines, reverse parking, signal discipline, and tight alley navigation.

RTO Licence Assistance

Step-by-step guidance for learner licence (LLR) and permanent driving licence (DL) application, documentation, and RTO test-track drill.

Why Choose Sridhar Motor Driving School

We Don't Just Teach You How to Steer. We Teach You How to Drive Safely.

Driving is a lifelong practical skill. Our training methodology emphasizes situational awareness, smooth clutch engagement, gentle braking, and understanding traffic flow.

Patient, Stress-Free Instruction

Learning to drive can trigger anxiety. Our instructors maintain a calm, encouraging demeanour with zero shouting, allowing you to build muscle memory at your natural pace.

1-on-1 Dedicated Behind-the-Wheel Time

You do not share the driver's seat with three other learners. Your scheduled hour is 100% focused on your practice and immediate feedback.

Dual-Control Safety Fleet

All instruction vehicles are fitted with approved dual safety pedals (instructor clutch and brake) so mistakes can be safely intercepted on public streets.

Real Bangalore Road Navigation

We move beyond empty grounds into realistic traffic scenarios—junctions, flyovers, roundabouts, and narrow residential lanes.

Flexible Batch Schedules

Morning batches start early from 6:00 AM before city rush hour; evening slots run up to 7:30 PM for office-goers and students.

Central Austin Town Location

Centrally located in Austin Town, Neelasandra, easily reachable from Victoria Layout, Richmond Town, Shanthi Nagar, and Ulsoor.
